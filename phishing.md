Phishing Awareness

Introduction
Phishing is a cybersecurity attack where criminals use fake emails, messages and websites to trick users into revealing confidential information.

Common Signs of Phishing
* Suspicious email addresses.
* Unexpected links and attachments.
* Messages requesting passwords.
* Urgent messages demanding immediate action.

Prevention
* Avoid clicking suspicious links.
* Verify email senders.
* Never share passwords.
* Report suspicious messages.

Conclusion
Users should remain alert and verify online communications to protect themselves against phishing attacks.
